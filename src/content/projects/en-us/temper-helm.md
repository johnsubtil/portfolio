## The problem

When you study on your own, you can take in a lot of information and feel like you've learned, yet **get stuck when it's time to build something**.

## The idea

The app starts with **a personal goal**, like "make my own game", and maps out what you need to learn. To show you've learned it, you need to build something and be able to use that knowledge beyond the original project.

## The method

Each plan, called a **forge**, starts with a goal, how much time you have and a deadline. It's then broken down into blocks and smaller items, each with a hands-on task and a way to tell when it's done.

## Where it stands

I'm still **researching the problem and working out the method**. I've revised the research once, checking sources and correcting figures. There's a lot I haven't decided about how the app will work yet. I'll add those details here as I work them out.
