## Why themes

The whole site had just one look, and I didn't want just one. Some days I want a lab, a golden kingdom or even a site full of memes (maybe one day?). So I decided to build a theme manager: the same content, with **lots of different atmospheres**.

## The two themes

I adapted the theme from the first version, **Golden Royal**, into something more fitting for royalty. But I didn't feel it really represented the way I wanted to express myself here.

That's when the idea for **Dark Lab** came up. I had already defined this site as a lab for experiments and projects, so why not have a lab theme too?

## A bit more technical

Each theme has its own CSS file, with its own effects and style. That way, if I ever want to add or remove a theme, I don't have to touch the rest of the site. The theme you pick is saved in your browser and applied before the page shows up.
