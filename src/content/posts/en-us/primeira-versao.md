Well, the **first version of the site** is done. You'll see the project marked as completed, but there's still plenty to improve and keep running.

## Building the site

It took a lot of coding, design work and back-and-forth with my buddy Claude Code. This was my first time working on the front end, so I learned a lot as I went. Some things worked, some didn't, and putting my own work out there was pretty intimidating.

Part of it was figuring out how to introduce myself and bring all my work together. Writing the descriptions took a while too. I wanted them to make sense and still sound like me. Some went through quite a few drafts before I settled on what you're reading now.

There are already two ongoing projects on the site. **Hawise** is a story that's been on my mind for years and started taking shape in 2026. **Temper Helm** is a tool I'm planning to help people dig deeper into what they're learning and put it to use in their own work.

## Next steps

Next, I want to replace the AI-generated vector covers with **my own pixel art** and **add a newsletter**. Pixel art is what I know how to make right now, but I'd like to try other kinds of 2D illustration as I get better.

For now, the site is live and I can start posting what I'm working on.

## A thank-you

And a big thank you to **Austin Kleon** for [*Show Your Work!*](https://austinkleon.com/show-your-work/). Without that book, I'd probably still be keeping all of this to myself.
